How to read a session

× 2 · 3 sets
Three sets of two reps at the weight shown.
2+1
A combination done as one set without dropping the bar, for example 2 cleans then 1 jerk.
3+3
On lunges, three reps on each leg.
Your pick
No set weight. Choose a load you can move with clean form.
Off blocks
The bar starts on blocks at about knee height.
2 halts
Pause briefly at the knee and again at mid-thigh.
Per side
Plates to load on each side of the bar, with collars.

Programs from Bob Takano, Weightlifting Programming: A Winning Coach's Guide, as transcribed in the Lift Vault spreadsheet. Level standards are the spreadsheet's qualification totals by bodyweight.

Source fixes: Class III week 4 Monday hang power snatch was garbled and is shown as 50×3, 60×3, 65×3 for 3 sets, then 70×2 for 2 sets. The CMS tab has no calculation sheet, so its percentages were converted the way the spreadsheet converts the other levels: snatch work as written, clean & jerk work +5 points, back squat ÷1.5 and front squat to the matching front-squat percentage. “Max” rows mean today's heavy single, not the max you entered.

If every set looks fast and easy, your maxes are probably set low. If you miss many reps, lower them by 5–10%. Ticks are saved in this browser only.
